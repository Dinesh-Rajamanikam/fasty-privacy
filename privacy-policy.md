# Privacy Policy for Fasty

**Last updated: October 26, 2026**

Fasty ("the app", "we", "us", or "our") is an intermittent-fasting timer and personal tracking app. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Fasty.

## Information we handle

Fasty stores the information you enter or generate while using the app, including your name (if provided), fasting plans, fasting-session dates and durations, streaks, achievements, notification preferences, schedule settings, and theme preference.

## Where your information is stored

This version of Fasty stores the information listed above locally on your device. Fasty does not operate an account system or a cloud service, and it does not transmit, sell, rent, or share your fasting information with us or with third parties.

If you use your device's app backup or transfer features, your device platform may handle app data according to its own privacy policy and your device settings.

## Notifications

If you allow notifications, Fasty schedules reminders and fasting-status notifications locally on your device. Notification permission is optional and can be changed at any time in your device settings or in the app.

## Third-party services and advertising

Fasty does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services in the current version. It does not use your fasting data for advertising or profiling.

## Data retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it through the app (where available), clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling the app generally removes locally stored app data; device backups may retain a copy until managed or deleted under your platform's backup rules.

## Children's privacy

Fasty is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children because the app does not collect or transmit personal information to us.

## Health disclaimer

Fasty is a tracking and motivational tool, not medical advice. Talk with a qualified healthcare professional before changing your diet or fasting routine, especially if you are pregnant, have a medical condition, have a history of disordered eating, or take medication.

## Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's data practices change. The current version will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
